# AJ3 Luxury Stay

Create a polished, premium, responsive marketing website concept for AJ3 LUXURY HOME, a furnished-apartment / serviced accommodation brand in Cameroon. This is a speculative proposal/demo, not an official site. Build a high-converting hospitality landing website, primarily in French (the brand's social bio is French), with a small English language toggle or concise English support if practical.

Brand and facts to use:
- Brand name: AJ3 LUXURY HOME
- Positioning: Appartements meublés haut standing
- Contact / WhatsApp numbers: +237 658 346 919 and +237 682 814 819
- Social handle: @aj3luxuryhome on TikTok
- Do NOT invent address, exact apartment count, room configurations, prices, availability, reviews, awards, or facilities. Where details are unknown, use neutral copy and clearly editable placeholders, or omit.
- Do not claim real-time booking. The main CTA should open WhatsApp with a prefilled inquiry message; secondary CTA should call the phone number. Link to TikTok profile using https://www.tiktok.com/@aj3luxuryhome.

Visual direction:
- Sophisticated boutique-hospitality aesthetic: warm ivory / sand backgrounds, deep charcoal typography, restrained muted gold accents, elegant serif display headlines paired with clean sans-serif body text, generous whitespace, editorial composition, subtle motion, refined borders.
- Use high-quality, realistic luxury apartment / interior photography as temporary illustrative imagery, with graceful overlays and good contrast. Make it clear through content structure that these are illustrative visuals to be replaced with AJ3's actual apartment photos before launch. Do not imply stock images are verified photos of their properties.
- Avoid generic template look, excessive gradients, glassmorphism, loud colors, fake badges, fake testimonials, fake ratings, fake booking widgets.

Site sections:
1. Sticky responsive navigation with logo wordmark, links Accueil, Appartements, Expérience, Galerie, Contact; prominent "Réserver" WhatsApp CTA; mobile menu.
2. Hero with large editorial luxury apartment image, eyebrow "SÉJOURS • CONFORT • ÉLÉGANCE", headline "Votre séjour, une expérience à part.", short value proposition and two CTAs "Réserver sur WhatsApp" and "Découvrir nos appartements".
3. Trust/value strip with only general honest messages such as "Accueil personnalisé", "Confort au quotidien", "Réservation simple" — no unsupported certifications.
4. Apartments / accommodation preview with 2-3 elegant image cards using neutral titles like "Espaces de vie", "Suites & chambres", "Détails & confort"; do not invent exact inventory or features. CTA "Demander les disponibilités".
5. Experience section focused on calm, comfort, privacy and service, with image + concise French copy. Avoid claiming specific amenities not confirmed.
6. Gallery grid using illustrative photos with a discreet note: "Visuels d'inspiration — à remplacer par les photos réelles des appartements AJ3 Luxury Home."
7. Strong booking/contact banner with WhatsApp and call CTAs.
8. Contact section with both phone numbers, TikTok, and a simple inquiry form (name, dates, number of guests, message) that when submitted opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message; do not store data or pretend a backend exists.
9. Footer with brand and navigation.

Conversion details:
- All WhatsApp CTAs must open https://wa.me/237658346919?text=... with relevant prefilled French inquiry text.
- Include a second call CTA using tel:+237682814819 (and show both numbers).
- Ensure CTAs work on desktop and mobile, accessibility, SEO title/description, good responsive layouts, fast-loading images.
- Make this look like a credible, premium concept that a web developer can show to the business owner as a proposal, not a generic booking platform.
- Build the actual website, not just a written plan.
